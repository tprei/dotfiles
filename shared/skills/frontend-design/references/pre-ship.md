# Pre-ship checklist

Includes modified guidance from Impeccable and Anthropic. See [sources](SOURCES.md).

Inspect the delivered artifact, not the plan or the source alone. Render a narrow phone (often 390px), useful intermediate widths, a representative wide view (often 1440px), landscape, tablet and the user's exact target where applicable. Include a 320px stress view on the web, the shipped device classes on native, and exact export size or terminal dimensions for artifacts. Batch a complete check per view, fix material failures together, and open a second set of captures. Verify keyboard, pointer and touch with interactions, not screenshots. Use device/tool evidence relevant to the actual renderer. Name missing evidence.

## Purpose, composition and copy

- [ ] The first view communicates whose surface this is, the mode's job, subject, current context and the next useful action. A five-second skim of headings, labels and values works.
- [ ] The initial lead is specific to the brief and its evidence or artifact, not a card-grid, huge-number, or filler-hero template. There is one dominant idea at a time. Every section has one job. Remove one unnecessary effect, container or string and see whether the hierarchy improves.
- [ ] Claims, names, logos, images, dates, units, proofs, and charts are truthful. Label samples, partial figures, and stale/unknown values. Provide origin for assets and a text/data alternative where the output format needs it.
- [ ] Buttons predict outcomes; errors identify the issue and recovery; labels, status and copy say each fact once. No throwaway taglines, prompt residue, redundant welcome, decorative punctuation, or forced slogan cadence.

## Craft, access and state

- [ ] Type faces and roles suit the subject; measure, leading, tracking, baseline, label fit and numerical alignment hold. Group related items tightly and separate groups with room, including near viewport and scroll edges. Any visible frame, shape, icon or shadow has a purpose.
- [ ] Palette roles are stable; computed text, controls and focus meet [contrast targets](color.md#check-perception-and-contrast) in real states and themes. Do not rely on hue. No unearned gradient, glowing halo, rainbow, accent stripe, double elevation, nested card, stacked icon tile, kicker, meaningless number or badge, ungrounded grid/stripe, fake illustration or faux dashboard chrome. Verify all relevant [catalog checks](slop-catalog.md), including baseline and overlap issues.
- [ ] No focus trap, invisible focus, invisible current state, hidden input under an IME, hover-only decision or impossible hot spot. Check roles, landmarks, labels, alt text, focus travel, dismiss/restore, keyboard shortcuts as appropriate, screen reader, safe areas, target sizes and 200% zoom. Browser text wrapping, RTL, and platform text scaling are real checks.
- [ ] Default, hover, focus, pressed, selected, disabled, loading, error, empty, stale, partial, success and permission states that apply are present. Long names, duplicates, realistic data, rapid actions, faults, offline, interrupted gestures, and navigation away do not leave success ambiguous or destroy work.
- [ ] Motion explains relationship or state, content is visible without it, and reduced motion retains meaning. No fake activity or repeated template entrances. Test noisy frames, start, end, interruption and repetition. Ambient effects stay bounded and optional.
- [ ] Real assets and fonts load, console/runtime failures are addressed, layout remains stable, and the interface stays usable on representative slow devices. The first screen remains a usable screen, not an inert picture. See [optimize](optimize.md).

## Independent finish pass

Give a fresh reviewer the request, confirmed constraints, current captures, artifact, brief, applicable baseline/comp, measurements, and existing findings. The reviewer opens rendered evidence before the builder's summary. If reviewing alone, separate the pass and disclose the limitation. Settle non-state entrance motion, start full-page captures at the top, and check dimensions, page, state, and completeness. Wrong, blank, partial and missing files do not support a verdict.

Inventory the approved reference where applicable and compare each region. Check type character, material, ground temperature and value, topology, reading order, scale, overlap, actual CTA behavior, truthful product content and the signature move. Mark each match, justified adaptation, missing, contradicted, or unauthorized addition. Cite the reason for each adaptation, such as accessibility, product truth, or responsive reflow. A code-led inspiration image sets finish, not composition. Check the world against its promise without hiding deviations beneath a high numeric score.

| Disposition | Next action |
|---|---|
| Recapture | Evidence is invalid. Recapture named views and repeat the full review; the earlier verdict is void. |
| Rebuild | Focal material or structure contradicts the brief wholesale. Re-derive those regions and assets and ask for a full review, not a patch verdict. |
| Fix | Order the material findings, apply one batch, recapture the same views, and score each finding resolved, partial or unresolved. Preserve what already works. |
| Ship | Valid evidence, no open material fixes in the reviewed scope. State the scope and how to open the result. |

Do not claim whole-surface approval from a fix-only verdict. Treat new user evidence as a new full review input. On a new or replacement identity, document the final implemented system and provenance after corrections; preserve the system on local refinements. In an unattended review allow one substantive correction round and report residual gaps. If running an attended round yields no improvement, stop repeating it; surface open issues and the choice they require.

## Supporting launch surfaces

When in scope, check the not-found route explains what happened and links to safety, favicon and app icon use the real mark, and appropriate share metadata includes a readable preview (around 1200×630). Use approved legal copy and never invent policy, contact information, account details, or claims as polish. Purposeful accessibility and reusable tokens are product foundations, not work to defer to decoration.
