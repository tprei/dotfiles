# Quieter

Includes modified guidance from Impeccable. See [sources](SOURCES.md).

Reduce stimulation without removing the surface's character or its anchors. Identify whether the noise is saturation, number of hues, competing contrasts, scale, bold weight, density, shape, texture, layering, or motion. Name the message and action that stay emphatic. Persuade and Experience should retain drama; Operate and Read should stop impeding the task.

- Bring strong colors toward roughly 70–85% of their previous saturation as a starting comparison, reduce variety, and let a dominant neutral leave the accent near 10% of the surface. Recheck ink and control contrast; quiet is not pale unreadable text. Use tinted secondary ink on colored grounds. See [color](color.md).
- Reduce gratuitous weight, for example 900 to 600 or 700 to 500 where the role stays legible. Shrink exaggerated size jumps, not body or touch areas. Give groups more room, align them to the grid, and cut borders or radius where the shape is competing for attention.
- Remove ornamental gradients, patterns, layered shadows, glows, and blur. Flatten ordinary panels. Preserve the one device that makes the surface recognizable.
- Reduce distance, for example 40px to 10–20px, or remove movement entirely if it has no job. Use understated ease-out rather than bounce; keep confirmations and focus apparent.

Compare before and after in the same view and path. Verify that scanning, extended reading, and access improve, the primary action and visual point of view survive, and the surface has not collapsed to uniform gray text in identical cards. Finish with [polish](polish.md).
