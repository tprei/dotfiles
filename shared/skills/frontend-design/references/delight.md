# Delight

Includes modified guidance from Impeccable. See [sources](SOURCES.md).

Choose a moment that merits a response: significant success, a wait that could become informative, discovery of real utility, first use, recovery, or mastery. Decide the feeling, product-specific reason, frequency and emotional stakes in one sentence. One unexpectedly good interaction, material cue, clear phrase, original illustration or permitted haptic can do more than widespread whimsy.

| Moment | Make it better |
|---|---|
| Success | Acknowledge effort or consequence. A small, certain response suits a routine save; reserve celebration for a real milestone. |
| Waiting | Show truthful progress, useful context or actual product activity. Never prolong completion to stage an effect. |
| First use | Make the next action clear before adding voice. Reveal genuine value as the user explores. |
| Error | Lead with what failed and how to recover. Use warmth without trivializing blocked work, payments, privacy or loss. |
| Repeat use | Try the hundredth use. Preserve quick, recognizable feedback; variation must not make a control less predictable. |
| Discovery | Reward curiosity with capability without hiding required functions behind exploration. |

Keep sound opt-in and honor mute, reduced motion, device settings, keyboard, assistive technology, and cultural meaning. Use haptics sparingly with the platform convention. Do not block tasks, add unsupported claims, or spend more bytes or frame time than the moment is worth. In Operate and Read, concentrate expression at earned moments. In Persuade and Experience, carry it through voice or material while keeping one focal hierarchy. Remove the flourish and confirm the base still works; restore it only if the product genuinely loses character or clarity. See [motion](motion.md), then [polish](polish.md).
